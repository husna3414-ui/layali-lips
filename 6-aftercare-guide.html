After Your Treatment

Lip Filler Aftercare & Recovery

A calm, complete guide to healing comfortably and protecting your result — what's normal, what's not, and exactly what to do.

Lips swell easily, so a little care in the first few days makes a big difference. The first day or two are not your final result — swelling settles, and your lips usually look their best by around two weeks.

First 24–48 hours

Please do

  • Gently apply a wrapped cool pack for short periods to ease swelling
  • Stay hydrated & eat soft foods
  • Keep lips clean
  • Sleep slightly propped up; avoid sleeping face-down
  • Take Paracetamol if you need it

Please avoid

  • Touching, pressing or massaging the lips (unless we've instructed it)
  • Makeup or lipstick on the lips
  • Hot drinks, spicy food & drinking through a straw
  • Alcohol, smoking & vaping
  • Strenuous exercise, saunas, steam, hot baths & sunbeds
  • Flying, kissing & dental work for a few days

What is completely normal?

Normal & expected

  • Swelling — often more the next morning
  • Bruising & tenderness
  • Small lumps or unevenness as it settles
  • Tightness or a "full" feeling
  • Mild asymmetry while swollen
  • Dryness or slight flaking of the lip skin
Don't judge your result for ~2 weeks. Swelling makes lips look bigger and sometimes uneven at first — this settles. Small lumps usually smooth out as the filler integrates.

What is NOT normal — contact us immediately

Lip filler is generally very safe, but a blocked blood vessel (vascular occlusion) is a rare emergency. Contact Layali Clinic straight away if you notice:
  • Severe or increasing pain that's out of proportion
  • Blanching — the skin around the lips turning white
  • Dusky, blue, grey or mottled discolouration of the lip or surrounding skin
  • Spreading redness, heat or swelling after the first few days, or pus / fever (signs of infection)
  • A cold sore developing
  • Any symptom that worries you

Healing timeline

Day 1–2

Swelling is at its peak (often worse in the morning), with possible bruising and tenderness.

Days 3–7

Swelling settles noticeably; any bruising fades; lips start to feel more like yours.

~2 weeks

Final result — lips fully settled and integrated. This is the time to assess and discuss any top-up.

A final reassurance

Most side effects are temporary and part of normal healing. By following this guide, the vast majority of clients heal beautifully and love their result once the swelling settles.
