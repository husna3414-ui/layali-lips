What is lip filler?

Lip filler is a hyaluronic acid (HA) dermal filler — a soft gel based on a sugar that occurs naturally in your skin. It's carefully injected into and around the lips to add volume, shape the lip border, balance symmetry and hydrate. Because it's HA-based, it is temporary and, if ever needed, can be dissolved.

How it works

Hyaluronic acid attracts and holds water, so it adds soft volume and structure while keeping the lips hydrated. Your practitioner places small amounts precisely to enhance your natural shape — building gradually rather than over-filling.

Realistic expectations — please read

We build lip filler gradually and conservatively for a natural result — more can always be added, but it shouldn't be rushed. Results are temporary (typically 6–12 months) and will need topping up over time. Lips are very expressive and naturally a little asymmetrical, so the aim is balance and beauty, not perfect symmetry. We'll always be honest at consultation about what will suit your face.

Who is it for?

May suit you if you

  • Want subtle, natural enhancement
  • Are over 18
  • Are in good general health
  • Have realistic expectations

Not suitable if you

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Have an active cold sore or infection
  • Have a known allergy to the product
  • Have certain autoimmune or bleeding conditions
  • Are unwell, or have a big event in the next 2 weeks

Suitability is always confirmed at a face-to-face consultation, and treatment may be declined if it isn't safe or appropriate.

What happens during treatment?

1. Consultation

We discuss your goals, assess your lips and face, review your medical history and agree a natural, balanced plan.

2. Numbing

A numbing cream (and filler often contains anaesthetic too) keeps you comfortable.

3. Treatment

Small amounts of filler are placed precisely with a fine needle or cannula. The appointment usually takes around 30 minutes.

4. After

You can head straight home. Expect some swelling — your aftercare guide explains exactly what to do.

What does it feel like?

Numbing keeps you comfortable; most clients feel mild pressure or a small scratch. Lips are sensitive, so some tenderness afterwards is normal.

Downtime & recovery

It's normal to have swelling (often more the next morning), some bruising, tenderness and small lumps as it settles. Most swelling eases within 24–72 hours, and your lips usually settle to their final result by about 2 weeks. Plan around any big events — avoid them for ~2 weeks.

Results & how long it lasts

You'll see a difference straight away, though swelling exaggerates it at first. The true result settles over ~2 weeks. Lip filler typically lasts 6–12 months depending on the product, the amount and your metabolism, after which a top-up maintains your look.

Risks & side effects

Lip filler is very popular and generally well tolerated, but it's an injectable treatment and carries real risks, which we explain fully (and you'll complete a medical history and consent form first).

Common & usually temporary

  • Swelling
  • Bruising
  • Tenderness
  • Small lumps or unevenness
  • Temporary asymmetry while settling

Less common / serious

  • Infection
  • Cold sore reactivation
  • Allergic reaction
  • Persistent lumps or nodules
  • Vascular occlusion — rare but serious (a blocked blood vessel)
  • Need to dissolve or further treatment

A vascular occlusion is a rare emergency — your practitioner is trained to recognise and manage it, and HA filler can be dissolved. Your aftercare guide lists the warning signs.

Frequently asked questions

Will it look natural?

Yes — we build gradually and conservatively so your lips look like a softer, more defined version of your own, not "done."

Does it hurt?

Numbing cream (and anaesthetic in the filler) keeps you comfortable; most feel only mild pressure. Some tenderness afterwards is normal.

How long does it last?

Typically 6–12 months, then a top-up maintains your result.

How much swelling will I have?

Swelling is normal for 24–72 hours and often looks bigger the next morning — it settles, with the final result by about 2 weeks.

Can it be reversed?

Yes — hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with an enzyme if needed.

When can I wear lipstick / go to an event?

Avoid makeup on the lips for 24 hours, and ideally plan around big events for ~2 weeks while it settles.
